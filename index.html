How to get an item back

UPSA Find helps students connect. Keep the return safe and protect private information.

  1. 1. Contact the right personUse the listing’s email button to contact the finder for a found item, or the owner for a lost item.
  2. 2. Verify it privatelyAsk the owner to describe a detail that was not posted. Do not publish or email passwords, PINs, full ID numbers, or private serial numbers.
  3. 3. Arrange a safe handoverMeet at an official UPSA security or lost-property office, if available. Otherwise choose a busy public place on campus, and do not go alone if you feel unsafe.
